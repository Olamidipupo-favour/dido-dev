
# Dido: Decentralised Donation Platform

## Project Overview:
DiDo is a revolutionary platform that leverages web3 technologies to foster transparency and credibility in the donation market. Built with innovation and user-centric design in mind, DiDo empowers users to donate and request donations with ease while ensuring the integrity and traceability of all transactions through blockchain technology.
### Technologies Used:
* Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* UI/UX Design: Figma
* Asset Design: Canva
* Backend: Flask (Python framework)
* Blockchain Deployment: Optimism Sepolia
* Subgraph: The Graph NB: This was only implemented on the backend to retrieve transactions as there wasn't enough time.
* Database: MongoDB
* Deployment: Koyeb(backend,  flask server), VPS(frontend)
* Version Control: GitHub
* IDE: Visual Studio Code (VS Code)
* Solidity Programming Language: Utilized for specific blockchain-related tasks and smart contract development.
* Web Assembly Language: Used for generating the Graph entities.
* GraphQL: Implemented to fetch queries from the deployed Subgraph API, enhancing data retrieval and manipulation capabilities.
* Optimism Sepolia
## Key Features:
* Transparent Transactions: All donations are stored on the blockchain, ensuring transparency and traceability of funds.
* User-Friendly Interface: Intuitive design and user-friendly interface make it easy for users to navigate and interact with the platform.
* Donation Requests: Users can easily request donations for their causes or projects, expanding the reach of charitable giving.
* Security and Scalability: DiDo prioritizes quality coding practices to ensure the efficiency, security, and scalability of the platform.
* Web3 Integration: Effective integration of web3 protocols and frameworks enables seamless interaction with blockchain technology.


## MVP Features:
* User Registration and Login: Secure user accounts for managing donations.
* Donation Functionality: Users can donate cryptocurrency to listed causes.
* Request Functionality: Users can submit requests for donations, outlining their needs and goals.
* Transaction Tracking: Users can view the history of their donations and requests on the blockchain.
* Cause Listing: A curated list of verified causes that users can support. (Optional in MVP, can be expanded later)
* Search Functionality: Users can search for specific causes or donation requests.

## Future Development:
* Integration of Fiat Currency Donations: We plan to enhance the donation platform by integrating support for fiat currency donations, enabling users to contribute using traditional currencies seamlessly.
* Implementation of a Reputation System: To foster trust and accountability within our ecosystem, we aim to implement a robust reputation system for causes and donors. This system will recognize and reward integrity, transparency, and active participation.
* Development of a Mobile Application: Recognizing the importance of accessibility, we are committed to developing a mobile application to facilitate on-the-go donations. This mobile application will provide users with a convenient and user-friendly platform to contribute to charitable causes anytime, anywhere.
* Expansion to Support Various Charitable Sectors: Our vision includes expanding the platform to cater to a wide range of charitable sectors. By diversifying our support, we aim to address a broader spectrum of social and humanitarian needs, thereby maximizing our impact on communities worldwide.
* Support for Emerging Developers: We will offer support to upcoming developers by providing ETH grants for deploying their smart contracts on Optimism Sepolia or building their 'Subgraph' on The Graph infrastructure. This initiative aims to foster innovation and encourage participation in the decentralized ecosystem.
* Transparency in Project Funding: Project founders will have the opportunity to launch transparent donation projects, where they can allocate funds to qualified causes or requests without partiality. This transparent approach ensures that funds are distributed equitably and following the intended purpose, fostering trust and confidence among stakeholders.
As we strive for continuous improvement and innovation, these initiatives represent our commitment to creating a more inclusive, transparent, and impactful donation platform for the betterment of society.
## Problems encountered:
* Smart Contract Verification: During the process of verifying the smart contract, our team encountered difficulties. Despite initial efforts, we faced obstacles that required consultation with experienced web3 developers present at the Hackathon. Through their guidance and expertise, we were able to overcome these challenges and successfully verify the smart contract.
* Deployment of Python Backend Server: The deployment of our Python backend server posed significant challenges. Python, known for its strict syntax and dependency on modules, presented complexities when deploying on various infrastructures such as Vercel, Render, Koyeb, and a Private VPS. Overcoming these hurdles demanded thorough troubleshooting and exploration of alternative deployment strategies to ensure the seamless operation of our backend infrastructure.


* Scalability Concerns: As our project progressed, concerns regarding scalability surfaced. Anticipating the potential influx of users and data growth, we encountered challenges in designing and implementing scalable solutions. Addressing these concerns necessitated a thorough assessment of our architecture and the implementation of scalable design patterns to accommodate future growth effectively.
* Security Vulnerabilities: Throughout the development lifecycle, identifying and mitigating security vulnerabilities posed ongoing challenges. Conducting comprehensive security audits and implementing robust security measures became imperative to safeguard our system against potential threats and vulnerabilities. Collaborating with security experts and adhering to best practices in secure development played a pivotal role in mitigating these risks effectively.





## Conclusion:
DiDo represents a groundbreaking innovation in the donation market, offering a transparent and credible platform for charitable giving. With its user-centric design, seamless web3 integration, and potential for community impact, DiDo is poised to revolutionize the way donations are made and received in the decentralized ecosystem.
